/* CULINA */

Culina is a self-hosted recipe app: your recipes, on your own server, for the
people you actually cook with.

/* TECHNOLOGY */

  Backend        C# / .NET, PostgreSQL, Dapper
  Frontend       SvelteKit, shipped as a progressive web app
  Delivery       One container holding both
  Languages      English, Deutsch

/* THANKS */

  To everyone who writes down a recipe so someone else can cook it.
